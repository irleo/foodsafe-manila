Reporting Disclosure for Private Testing

1. What the report contains

Use simulated symptoms and fictional suspected food/source and exposure details in this private trial. Do not enter genuine health information or another person's personal details. A report includes symptoms, food/source and exposure descriptions, report and submission dates, case count, your account reference, district/barangay, and precise device coordinates. Staff review may add investigation, classification, and surveillance-related fields. Even fictional entries may identify a tester when linked to an account or location.

2. Purpose and use

Reports support testing submission, review, investigation, geographic workflows, and aggregated report analytics. Citizen reports are kept separate from official case datasets; submitting a report does not automatically include it in official surveillance totals, outbreak thresholds, or prediction training. The app also displays analytics and predictions from backend datasets, which may contain simulated or incomplete information in this trial. Do not use this test app for diagnosis, treatment, or emergency decisions.

3. Access and recipients

Authorized testing staff may access reports through role-controlled backend interfaces. Reports are stored in MongoDB on the configured hosting infrastructure. Address lookup may use platform geocoding services and map tiles are loaded from OpenStreetMap. These services may process location or network information. Ask your test organizer for current hosting/provider details and any processing outside the Philippines. No government or institutional affiliation is implied.

4. Retention and choices

The testing period is arranged with your organizer. Automatic deletion of accounts and reports is not currently implemented; records remain until the organizer deletes them. Ask about retention and backups before providing identifiable information. Contact the organizer through your agreed testing channel for access, correction, deletion, or withdrawal of reporting consent. Signing out, stopping testing, or disabling location does not delete existing reports.

5. Separate reporting consent

Reporting is optional. Terms acceptance and Privacy Policy acknowledgement do not give consent to reporting or location processing. The controls below record acknowledgement of the reporting and location disclosures and purpose-specific consent for the simulated report, account reference, and precise location. Android or iOS controls device location access; there is no separate in-app location toggle or checkbox. When phone permission is granted and location services are enabled, the app automatically displays your location. You may decline and continue using other features.

Your reporting acknowledgement/consent version, location notice version, and server timestamp are saved with your account and referenced on submitted reports. Once accepted, this disclosure applies to later reports under the same versions; it is shown for acceptance again when the disclosure or applicable processing changes. Review the Terms of Use and Privacy Policy for the wider account and testing context.
